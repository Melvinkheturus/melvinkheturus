There are actually three diﬀerent writing skills that people often lump together as "copywriting." If
you master all three, your GitHub, portfolio, landing pages, LinkedIn, and even sales pitches become
dramatically stronger.
1. Storytelling (Why should I care?)
Storytelling gives context and emotion. It answers:
What problem existed? What journey happened? What changed?
Instead of:
I built an AI CRM using Next.js.

Sales teams often lose promising leads because customer conversations are scattered across
spreadsheets, emails, and messaging apps. I built MergeX Sales OS to bring every interaction
into a single workﬂow, helping teams spend less time searching for information and more
time closing deals.
Notice the diﬀerence?
The ﬁrst tells what.

Every interaction is designed to reduce friction. From instant search to keyboard shortcuts,
